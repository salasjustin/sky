# SKY-124 revision 3: the beats

The film follows Justin's storyboard literally, in one continuous camera move from the desk up into the cloud and back down. Each beat names what the viewer sees and what it means, and every beat reads from its own labels without explanation.

| Time        | What the viewer sees                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | What it means                                                                                                             | What makes it readable                                             |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0 – 5.2     | Four robots stand around one computer. Chips labelled "build", "lint" and "test" fly from three of them onto its screen, joining a "claude code" window, and a CPU and MEM monitor appears.                                                                                                              | A bunch of bots building stuff on one computer.                                                                           | The window titles and "Your computer" on the desk                  |
| 5.2 – 12    | The CPU and MEM bars go red, an orange glow and heat shimmer build around the monitor, the windows jitter and stutter, then grey out behind a frozen spinner and "Not responding". The robots scowl, sweat and steam.                                                                                    | The computer is overloaded, hangs and stops responding.                                                                   | The red bars and "Not responding"; the bots' faces                 |
| 12 – 23.5   | The lead robot gets an idea ("!") and throws a "task" card up; the camera follows it to a cloud labelled "Sky". A "sprite" appears, runs "Claude Code", "CI" and "tests", and a green check lands on each. The camera pulls back: the computer's bars drop to idle, its glow fades and the robots relax. | The bots hand the work to Sky, which starts a sprite that runs Claude Code, CI and tests, and the computer is free again. | The "Sky" and "sprite" labels, the chip names and the green checks |
| 23.5 – 30.5 | Update bubbles fly down from the cloud to the robots: "Needs approval?" with an "Approve" button that a robot taps (it turns into a check), then "Tests passed", then "PR ready". The robots hop each time one lands.                                                                                    | Sky keeps the bots updated and asks for approval, then the work continues to a pull request.                              | The text in each bubble                                            |
