# SKY-124 revision 2: three storylines

All three follow one prompt for one repo until it comes out as a pull request. Silent music-wise; effects only on the motion.

## A. The prompt that leaves the laptop (picked)

- **Device and form:** one object across worlds, shot as one continuous take. The camera never cuts: it follows a prompt card off a straining laptop, up into Sky, through a sandbox's glass, and back out across many sandboxes to the close.
- **Sees and feels:** the developer's laptop strains under the job (fan, pinned meters, a receipt growing per token); the card lifts away and the laptop goes quiet. In Sky the card drops into its own sandbox on a Claude subscription, the agent works the branch, stalls, asks a person in chat, and carries on. The work comes out as a pull request with a private preview link that opens the running app, and the field of sandboxes and merged pull requests shows it happens at scale.
- **First two seconds:** tight on a prompt box mid-typing on a laptop screen, key ticks, then the camera pulls back as Enter is pressed.
- **Beats:** type → send → strain → card lifted by a cursor → ascent through the horizon → drop into a sandbox → push through the glass → branch, stall, chat, reply → PR → link click → browser iris → pull back to many sandboxes → tilt up to 82 merged pull requests → the lockup.
- **Look:** two worlds on one vertical axis, a dark desk below and a lit dusk sky above, layered parallax, soft light, glass and UI surfaces; brand palette only, pain shown by what objects do.
- **Sound:** no music; effects cut to the picture: key ticks, clicks, paired whooshes on big moves, pops on landings, glitch hits on the pinned meters, one stretched riser into the close.

## B. Before and after, split then collapsing (dropped)

- **Device and form:** life before and after, as a split screen that collapses to one pane. The old way (laptop churning) plays left while the Sky way (sandbox, chat, PR) plays right, and the left pane shrinks away.
- **Why dropped:** two things play at once, so one piece of work doesn't travel through the film, and it reads as the card-and-panel layout the last cut was rejected for.

## C. The pull request first, then back to where it came from (dropped)

- **Device and form:** the result first, as an object-to-object chain. It opens on the finished preview running, then pulls back through the PR, the branch, the sandbox and the prompt, ending at the laptop.
- **Why dropped:** the problems become a reversed explanation instead of something that happens to the viewer, and the pain has less to push against.
