# Sound effects

Every file here is a Kenney sound from the Interface Sounds pack (1.0), under Creative Commons Zero (CC0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), copied unmodified. The pack's own `LICENSE.txt` says "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0)" and that the sounds are free for personal, educational and commercial use. Credit is not required.

- Pack: Interface Sounds 1.0, by Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
- Pack URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

kenney.nl refuses scripted downloads (HTTP 403), so the pack was taken from this public repackaging of the same CC0 pack, whose `LICENSE.txt` was read: https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds

In the film, the `open_*` files (a noise that swells) and the `close_*` files (a noise that decays) are paired into whooshes, slowed with `data-playback-rate`. One `open_004.wav` at 0.2x is the riser into the close. The `error_*` files are the strained buzz and the "Not responding" buzz.
