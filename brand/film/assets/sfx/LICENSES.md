# Sound effects

Every file here is a Kenney sound under Creative Commons Zero (CC0, http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), copied unmodified. Each pack's own `LICENSE.txt` says "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0)" and that the sounds are free for personal, educational and commercial use. Credit is not required.

kenney.nl refuses scripted downloads (HTTP 403), so the packs were taken from these public repackagings of the same CC0 packs, whose `LICENSE.txt` files were read:

- Interface Sounds 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds, from https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds
- UI Audio, https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio, from https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-ui-audio

In the film, the `open_*` files (a noise that swells) and the `close_*` files (a noise that decays) are paired into whooshes, slowed with `data-playback-rate`. One `open_004.wav` at 0.2x is the riser into the close.
